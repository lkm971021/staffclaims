# Service Director guide

1. Sign in. **Approvals** lists the claims Finance has already verified, with the amount for approval.
   - Amber: waiting longer than the set number of days.
   - Purple "special approval": the claim includes categories such as equipment, entertainment or gifts.
2. Open a claim. You will see:
   - each expense, its Finance outcome and the verified amount
   - Finance's comments and any duplicate decisions
   - on the right, the receipt for the selected line (click a line number to switch)
   - **Claim summary PDF** and **All supporting documents (PDF)**, for the full set
3. Decide:
   - **Approve**: the claim goes to Finance for payment, and the staff member is notified.
   - **Request clarification**: write your question. The claim goes back to Finance, who will follow up.
   - **Reject**: a reason is required. This is final.
4. **My recent decisions** shows your history.
5. **Overview** shows management charts: monthly trend, by branch, by category and by staff.

You cannot change receipts or Finance's verification records, and you cannot approve your own claims. Your own claims are finalised by Finance verification, per the company rule.
