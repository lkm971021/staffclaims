# Staff guide: submitting a claim on your phone

## Install the app (one time)

- **iPhone (Safari):** open the app link → tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** open the link → tap **⋮** → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

Then sign in with **Continue with Google**, using the email you gave Finance.

## Submit a claim

1. Tap **+ NEW EXPENSE CLAIM**. Choose the month the expenses belong to, then tap **Start claim**.
2. Tap **+ Add expense** for each receipt.
3. Under *Receipt / invoice*, tap one of:
   - **Take photo**: the camera opens. Photograph the receipt flat, in good light.
   - **Gallery**: pick photos you already have. You can choose several.
   - **PDF file**: for e-invoices or emailed receipts.
4. Adjust the photo:
   - Yellow corners appear around the receipt. Drag them to the receipt's edges if needed.
   - **Enhanced** makes faint receipts clearer.
   - Tap **Use page**.
   - Long receipt? Take several photos. They are combined into one PDF.
5. The app reads the receipt and fills in what it can (shop, date, amount, receipt no.). **Always check these values.** Automatic reading can make mistakes.
6. Complete the details:
   - Category
   - Description: what you bought
   - **Business purpose**: why the company needed it
   - Location, for parking, toll and petrol
   - The branch the cost belongs to
   - How you paid
7. Tap **Save expense**. Repeat for every receipt.
8. Tap **Submit to Finance** and confirm.

**Drafts are saved automatically.** You can come back to a draft any time before the deadline.

## Deadlines

Claims for a month must be submitted **by the 5th of the following month**. Payment is targeted for the **16th**, after Finance verification and Director approval. For example, June claims are due on 5 July and paid by 16 July.

## After you submit

- Track the status on your home screen: *Submitted → Finance verified → Director approved → Paid*.
- If Finance **returns** a claim, you get a notification. Open the claim, read Finance's comment, fix it and **Resubmit**. Your earlier submission is kept on record.
- When it is paid, you can see the payment date and reference.

## Tips that avoid rejections

- Photograph the **original receipt or invoice**. A bank or e-wallet screenshot only proves payment, not the expense.
- Make sure the shop name, date, items and total are all visible.
- Keep the **original hard copy** and hand it to Finance.
- From **July 2026**, ask the supplier for an **e-Invoice** where available, and fill in the e-Invoice details if shown.
- The app warns you about problems (for example, the amount doesn't match the receipt). Please check before submitting.
